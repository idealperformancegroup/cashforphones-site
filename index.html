Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you really buy financed phones?

Yes. A phone that's still being paid off is worth less than one that's paid off, because the carrier can block it if the balance isn't paid. Choose "Still paying it off" in the quote and the price already reflects that.

What if my screen is cracked or it won't turn on?

We still buy it. Cracked screens, cracked backs and devices that won't power on all get an offer. Choose the matching condition in the quote.

Can I mail my phone in?

Yes, from anywhere in the US. Lock in your price, choose "Mail it in", and we'll text you the shipping details. When it arrives we inspect it, send you the final price, and pay you.

What do I need to bring?

The device, a valid photo ID, and your passcode or account login so we can sign you out together. Bring the charger or box if you have them.

What happens to my photos and data?

Back up anything you want to keep first. When we meet, we sign out of your account and factory reset the device with you there.

Is the online price guaranteed?

Your online price is guaranteed for 10 minutes. Lock it in with your name and number and it's yours. If the device matches what you told us, that's what we pay. If something is different, we'll show you and give you the updated price before you decide.

Are you part of Apple or Samsung?

No. Cash for Phones Louisiana is an independent, locally owned buyer. We're not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple, Samsung, Google or any carrier.
